# Paragraph-level template

## Paragraph [ID] — [section/page]

1. **Central point:** What does the paragraph establish?
2. **Sentence roles:** Explain the function of each sentence.
3. **Logical links:** Contrast, addition, cause, evidence, qualification, interpretation, or transition.
4. **Argument structure:** Describe the actual sequence used by the author.
5. **Reusable language:** Select the most useful expressions and patterns.
6. **Three-mechanism practice:** Near-synonym discrimination, Chinglish revision, and active imitation.
7. **Progress marker:** Record the exact next sentence to study.

Do not force all paragraphs into one universal structure.
