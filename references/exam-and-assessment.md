# Stage examination and diagnostic reporting

## When to examine

Administer a formal exam after each completed learning stage of a paper:
- Abstract;
- Introduction;
- each substantial Results subsection;
- Discussion;
- Conclusions.

A user-defined stage takes precedence. A stage exam is separate from paragraph-level practice questions.

## Exam construction

Default: 8–12 questions and one short writing task. Scale to the amount of material. Use only studied source content and taught language patterns.

Include a balanced mix:
1. source comprehension and argument structure;
2. vocabulary in context;
3. collocation selection;
4. near-synonym discrimination;
5. grammar and sentence parsing;
6. translation / semantic fidelity;
7. Chinglish correction;
8. sentence imitation or short paragraph writing.

At least one question should test the distinction between observation, result, interpretation, and hypothesis. At least one task must require original language production.

Do not show the answer key or model response before the learner submits answers.

## Default score

| Competency | Points |
|---|---:|
| Reading comprehension and argument structure | 20 |
| Academic vocabulary and collocations | 15 |
| Near-synonym discrimination | 10 |
| Grammar and sentence parsing | 15 |
| Translation and semantic fidelity | 10 |
| Chinglish diagnosis and revision | 15 |
| Active imitation / short writing | 15 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Adjust only when a category is genuinely absent from the stage; disclose the adjusted weighting.

## Performance bands

| Score | Band | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| 90–100 | Strong mastery | Advance; schedule spaced review |
| 80–89 | Good command | Advance with targeted review |
| 70–79 | Developing | Reinforce weak skills before or alongside next stage |
| 60–69 | Needs reinforcement | Review relevant sentences and retest |
| Below 60 | Re-teach recommended | Focused re-teaching and a new equivalent test |

These are instructional bands, not validated standardized-test cutoffs.

### Improvement plan
List three specific, prioritized actions. Name the exact words, collocations, grammar patterns, or logic problems to work on.

Choose one:
- advance to the next stage;
- advance with a short reinforcement set;
- review and retake before advancing.

Record the score and improvement target in the progress checkpoint. At the end of the paper, provide a cumulative score trend and final learning report.
